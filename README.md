The Last Wok: a complete original HTML5 cooking game in hand-drawn 16-bit pixel art — warm tropical palette, wood counters, yellow lamps, steam, sunset light, tin plates, a sleeping cat and a ceiling fan. Character sprites generated by the author (game/sprites/*.png, 21 original pixel-art characters); the kitchen, food icons and effects are drawn in code on canvas — no copied art. One night, a fire took Rani's little warung — everything burned except one wok, still warm in the ashes. The neighbours came back the next morning, each carrying one ingredient, and asked her to cook again. So Rani lit the last wok, and every dish since is a thank-you. Run the warung during the lunch rush: read the order tickets, chop on the board, cook in the wok, grill satay, stack dishes on the plate, and serve before customers walk out. Head Chef AI, a strict rule-based head chef, barks the best next step, praises fast serves, and warns about the grumpy customer — and highlights the right button with a glow. Two modes: Rush (150-second timed shift, 3 stars) and Chill (no timer, no fail). No ads, no in-app purchases, no lives, no energy — every upgrade is earned with coins from serving food. Static files only, works offline.

## The name — one panel, then cooking

The Last Wok (Wajan Terakhir) — "The wok that refused to go out." Before the title screen, exactly one short panel tells the whole backstory, skippable with a button (or Enter): "One night, Rani's little warung burned down. Everything was lost — except one wok, still warm in the ashes. She lit it again. Every dish since is a thank-you." That is the whole story scene — about five seconds of reading, then title → mode → play, no forced reading. The rest of the story lives in one-line backgrounds for the regulars — tap the small "i" on any order ticket (or Sam the Guard) to read theirs. The game stays a cooking game first.

- **Ale** — "Orders mie ayam every morning. Quiet, thoughtful, still figuring life out — but this broth tastes like hope." A big, quiet man who always orders Mie Ayam (Chicken Noodles) and rambles gently while waiting ("Still figuring life out…", "One bowl at a time.", "Some days I wonder why I keep going... nobody seems to care.", "What is my purpose in life?", "This mie ayam is so good... it makes me want to live again."). Serve him well and on time and he smiles for the first time — "See you tomorrow." — the "Ale Smiled Today!" achievement. Ale is inspired by *"Seporsi Mie Ayam Sebelum Mati"* by Brian Khrisna (Grasindo, 2025); his line "I feel like a supporting character in my own story" is a translation of the book's "Aku hanyalah pemeran pembantu, bahkan di cerita kehidupanku sendiri." (p.9).
- **Mr Grumpy** — "Grumpy, but the most loyal. He and his wife always dreamed of a child." His wife **Mrs Grumpy** — "His wife of 40 years. She calms him down — and carries a quiet wish of her own." When little Nara is seated, his face softens and his patience drains slower, and he says quietly: "In another life, maybe we'll have a child of our own, dear." — "Maybe, dear. Maybe."
- **The Ayu family** (Mrs Ayu, Mr Dedi & little Nara, 6) — "Brings her daughter here every Sunday. The warung is their little tradition." Nara holds her spoon up like a treasure: "This is yummy!"
- **The old couple** (Mr Harto & Mrs Lastri) — "Neighbours who brought ingredients the morning after the fire."
- **The young group** — "Regulars since day one — they laugh, share food, and always come back."
- **Sam the Guard** — "Smiles at every customer. His kids' school fees and a third baby on the way keep him standing tall." He patrols a small loop beside the door (never blocking it) and greets every arrival.

## A warung that feels alive

The warung is calm and cozy, not a neon arcade: warm wood, golden light, plants, steam from the food, a cat asleep in the corner, and soft WebAudio ambience (gentle plucks + chatter murmur, M to mute). Customers are characters with a life, not faceless tickets:

- **The young group** (Raka & Dina) — hanging out, laughing ("haha, so tasty!"), sharing food, tipping well.
- **The old couple** (Mr Harto & Mrs Lastri) — slow and unhurried, hearts and smiles, eating together.
- **Sam the Guard** — the smiling security guard beside the door who patrols a small loop (never blocking the entrance) and greets every arriving customer ("Welcome in!"). Talk to him and he tells you, warmly, about his kids' school fees and the third baby on the way.
- **A TV on the wall** — always on Indonesian news, with a scrolling ticker of verified headlines (the VP resignation, the court ruling, the two new VP candidates). When a big headline breaks, someone glances up: Mr Grumpy grumbles ("Hmph. Politicians."), the old couple sighs, Sam nods thoughtfully.
- **The grumpy customer** (Mr Grumpy) — impatient, crossed arms, complains ("So slow!", "This is cold!"), drains patience 1.7x faster and tips little. Serve him correctly and on time for a score bonus and the rare **"Grumpy Customer Pleased!"** achievement. But when little Nara is seated nearby his face softens, his patience drains slower, and he speaks one quiet line to his wife. He is visually distinct from cheerful Mr Harto (blue shirt + black peci cap, laughing) — dark grey shirt, crossed arms, frown.
- **The Ayu family** — Mrs Ayu, Mr Dedi and six-year-old Nara arrive together and share one table; Nara holds her spoon up like a treasure and says "This is yummy!" when served well.
- **Head Chef AI** — the strict head chef: "HURRY! Customer 2 is waiting!", "Great, keep it up!", "Careful, that customer is grumpy!".

Customers chat with each other between orders, get visibly impatient with a sigh ("Huh…") instead of a harsh red alarm, and in Chill mode they never walk out angry — they just chat longer. No character has one line: every regular carries 6–8 different lines plus small pools for greetings, impatience, rainy days and evenings — and no line repeats twice in a row, so no two days sound the same. Seated neighbours trade short two-line exchanges that never repeat back-to-back, Chef AI cheers every serve with a fresh praise, Sam greets each arrival differently, and Bu Rani herself speaks when she serves ("Hot from the last wok!"). Rani walks the room on WASD / arrows or tap-to-move, and a grid pathfinder routes her around counters and tables, so a tap always arrives — she never gets wedged against a table. Twelve original warung dishes (nasi goreng, mie ayam, gado-gado, sate, ayam geprek, bakso, ayam goreng, kopi tubruk, jus alpukat, es teh…), combo scoring, coin-bought stove/decor upgrades, day/shift progression, tutorial, and win/lose screens.

## Run the warung like a manager (v4)

Every shift is one day at the warung — open until 2 AM — and every day ends at the **Morning Market**:

- **Buy ingredients** — every ingredient costs coins (Rice 2, Chicken 4, Satay Skewers 5, Iced Tea 1…) and cooking spends 1 unit from the pantry. A dish always sells for more than its ingredients cost, so a well-run day earns — but an empty pantry cannot cook that dish until you restock. Stock is shown on every ingredient card during play.
- **Hire Head Chef AI** — 40 coins for one day: he auto-cooks the most urgent ingredient on the busiest station while you play. His free hints stay free; hiring only adds the auto-help.
- **Kitchen upgrades & decor** — the same coin-bought upgrades (Sharp Knife, Non-Stick Wok, Double Burner, Happy Customers) and free wall colours, right in the market.
- **Three kitchen tools** — Wok, Chopping Board, and Grill (satay skewers). Chill mode is the relaxed management mode: no timer, no failing, no angry walk-outs — just slower tips.
- **Walk in, sit down, eat, leave** — every customer visibly walks in from the door (Sam the Guard waves), sits down as their ticket pops with a "!" bubble, eats happily with coins flying up when served, then walks back out. If patience runs out, they stand up annoyed and walk out. The entrance never blocks cooking — tickets are tappable from the first second.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080
```

Or open `game/index.html` through any static file server. Click to play: walk with WASD / arrow keys or tap a spot to walk there, press E (or tap) near a station to cook, near a table to take an order or serve the finished plate. K toggles Head Chef AI, P pauses, M mutes.
