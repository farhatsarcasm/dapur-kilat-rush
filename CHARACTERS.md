# The Last Wok — Character Sheet
## For AI image generation (pixel sprites) — v4

**Art direction for EVERY prompt below (paste this at the end of each prompt):**
`16-bit hand-drawn pixel art, warm tropical palette, soft sunset light, chunky pixels, cozy Indonesian warung style, in the style of A Space for the Unbound, original character, full-body sprite, transparent background, crisp pixels, no text, no watermark`

**Sprite specs:** 64x64 px grid per frame. Each character needs 3 poses: (1) walking in from the door, side view, (2) sitting at a table, front view, (3) happy/eating. Keep the same outfit and colours in all 3 poses.

**How the sprites will be used in the game:** each customer walks in through the door, walks to a table, sits down, orders, eats, then leaves. Staff stand in the kitchen / at the door.

## STAFF

### 1. Bu Rani — Owner & Head Cook
- **Role:** The player character / owner of the warung. She cooks at the wok.
- **Look:** Indonesian woman in her 40s, hair in a low bun with a batik headscarf, warm brown skin, green apron over a batik shirt, wooden sandals. Always holding a spatula.
- **Ciri khas (recognisable at a glance):** Bun + batik headscarf, green apron, always holding a spatula. Never panics — the wok does the talking.
- **Background:** Her little warung burned down one night. Everything was lost — except one wok, still warm in the ashes. She lit it again the next morning. Every dish since is a thank-you. That is why the warung is called The Last Wok.
- **Personality:** Warm, calm, stubborn. Never raises her voice; the wok does the talking.
- **Prompt:** `Indonesian woman cook in her 40s, low bun with batik headscarf, green apron over batik shirt, holding a wooden spatula, standing at a wok station,`

### 2. Chef AI (Koki AI) — Robot Sous-Chef
- **Role:** Head Chef AI. A small robot that suggests the next best action, highlights the right ingredient with a glow, and can be hired to auto-cook one station.
- **Look:** Small friendly robot, round white body, one big glowing eye-screen, a tiny chef hat, a screen on its chest that says "CHEF AI". Rolls on a single wheel.
- **Ciri khas (recognisable at a glance):** Round white robot, one big glowing eye-screen, tiny chef hat, "CHEF AI" written on its chest, rolls on one wheel.
- **Background:** Built from spare parts found in the ashes after the fire. Rani calls it "the voice of the wok" — strict, because the wok survived and expects the same from everyone.
- **Personality:** Strict but helpful. Barks orders, then praises good work.
- **Prompt:** `Small friendly robot sous-chef, round white body, one big glowing eye screen, tiny chef hat, chest screen showing CHEF AI, rolling on one wheel,`

### 3. Sam the Guard — Security Guard at the Door
- **Role:** Greets every customer who walks in. Stands BESIDE the door (never in the doorway — the entrance must stay clear so customers can walk in freely). Sometimes patrols slowly inside the warung.
- **Look:** Tall friendly man, dark blue security uniform with a cap, big warm smile, moustache.
- **Ciri khas (recognisable at a glance):** Tall and big, dark blue uniform + cap, moustache, always smiling and waving — standing beside the door, never blocking it.
- **Background:** The neighbourhood security guard. He has stood at that door since the warung reopened and greets every single person by name. Behind his smile he carries a heavy load: his children need school fees to keep studying, and his wife is pregnant with their third child. He works hard and smiles because of his family — proud and hopeful, never pitiful.
- **Personality:** The friendliest person in the game. Warm, proud, hopeful. "Welcome in!"
- **Lines:** "My kids' school fees are due this month — every smile helps.", "My wife is expecting our third. I work so they can dream bigger.", "Tired? A little. But the warung feels like family."
- **Prompt:** `Tall friendly Indonesian security guard, dark blue uniform and cap, big warm smile, moustache, standing beside a restaurant door waving, entrance kept clear,`

### 4. Mochi — The Living Cat
- **Role:** Mascot. Alive, not just sleeping in a corner — walks slowly around the warung, stretches, sits in sun patches, tail swishing. Wakes up / meows with a heart bubble when a big tip lands.
- **Look:** Small orange tabby cat, pink nose, tail held high when walking.
- **Ciri khas (recognisable at a glance):** Small orange tabby, pink nose, always moving slowly — never just a static prop.
- **Background:** A stray Rani fed the morning after the fire. It never left.
- **Rain behaviour:** When it rains, Mochi moves to sleep curled up by a window, watching the rain — the quiet, calm moment of the warung.
- **Prompt:** `Small orange tabby cat walking slowly in a cozy kitchen, pink nose, tail up, curious and calm,`

## CUSTOMERS

### 5. Ale — The Quiet Thinker (signature order: Mie Ayam / Chicken Noodles)
- **Role:** Regular. Always orders mie ayam, sits alone, rambles softly about life while waiting.
- **Look:** Big man, 37, tall and heavy (189 cm), dark skin, short black hair, plain dark grey shirt, tired eyes, slumped shoulders. Drawn larger than other customers.
- **Ciri khas (recognisable at a glance):** The biggest body in the room, dark skin, plain dark grey shirt, tired eyes, slumped shoulders, always sitting alone with his mie ayam.
- **Background:** Inspired by the novel "Seporsi Mie Ayam Sebelum Mati" by Brian Khrisna (Grasindo, 2025). A quiet man who feels like a supporting character in his own story. Nobody talks to him at work — only the office boy greets him. The one decision that was truly his own was choosing a bowl of mie ayam. In our warung he is heard: when served well and on time, he smiles for the first time and says he will come back tomorrow.
- **Lines:** "Still figuring life out…", "One bowl at a time.", "Some days I wonder why I keep going... nobody seems to care.", "What is my purpose in life?", "This mie ayam is so good... it makes me want to live again." (tears, then a small smile), "This broth tastes like hope."
- **Prompt:** `Big quiet Indonesian man, tall and heavy build, dark skin, short black hair, plain dark grey shirt, tired kind eyes, sitting alone at a small table with a bowl of chicken noodle soup, thoughtful expression,`

### 6. Mr Grumpy — The Grumpy Regular
- **Role:** First customer after the fire. Crossed arms, taps the table, complains — but never misses a day.
- **Look:** Older man, grey hair, thick eyebrows, dark grey shirt, arms crossed, frown.
- **Ciri khas (recognisable at a glance):** Grey hair, thick eyebrows, dark grey shirt, arms always crossed, tapping the table, frowning.
- **Background:** The very first customer who came back after the fire. Grumpy, impatient, low tips — but the most loyal customer the warung has. ## WALK-IN / SIT-DOWN ANIMATION (for the game)
Every customer uses the same 3-beat entrance, like popular pixel cooking games:
1. **Walk in** — spawn at the door (right side), walk left to their table, 2-step walk cycle, Sam the Guard waves.
2. **Sit down** — settle into the chair, order ticket pops above their head with a small "!" bubble.
3. **Eat & leave** — when served, happy eating animation (spoon up and down), coins fly up, then they stand, walk back to the door, and leave. If patience runs out, they stand up annoyed and walk out.

Keep the cooking first: the entrance never blocks cooking, and the player can always tap ingredients while a customer walks in.

## WARUNG HIDUP — CHAT BUBBLES & EXPRESSIONS (v4)
Every character looks alive, not just present:
- **They talk to each other, not only to the player.** Chat bubbles appear in turn, briefly, then fade — cooking keeps going. Young group laughing together ("Best satay, I want more!" / "Right??" / "Next round on me!!"), the old couple talking quietly ("Of course... since 1975!"), Ale talking to himself while a neighbour nods, Mr Grumpy grumbling while Mrs Grumpy calms him ("He just needs more food, Pak."). And one quiet moment: when little Nara is seated, Mr Grumpy says to his wife, "In another life, maybe we'll have a child of our own, dear." — "Maybe, dear. Maybe."
- **Expressions are visible.** Happy/laughing when served well, thinking/worried when patience runs low, annoyed when an order takes too long, satisfied while eating, surprised when a random event arrives. Mr Grumpy has one more face: soft — a sad little smile when a child is seated nearby. Mood is readable from their face, not just from numbers.
- **The two old couples stay distinct.** Mr & Mrs Grumpy (dark grey shirt, crossed arms, frown; brown kebaya, grey bun) are never the cheerful Harto couple (blue shirt + black peci cap, laughing; grey bun + patterned skirt). Shirt colour + peci cap + posture tell them apart at a glance.
- **Staff are alive too.** Chef AI praises a good dish, Sam greets every arrival, Bu Rani smiles when a customer is happy, Mochi meows / shows a heart bubble on a big tip.
- **A TV on the wall, always on.** Indonesian news with a scrolling ticker (verified headlines, English, neutral — the VP resignation, the court ruling, the two new VP candidates). When a big headline breaks, someone glances up: Mr Grumpy grumbles "Hmph. Politicians.", the old couple sighs, Sam nods thoughtfully.
- **The room is bigger with more windows** (per the user's 2026-11-25 direction): more space to walk around, rain visible through the windows, warm indoor light against it — and Mochi sleeps by a window when it rains.
